Voice cloning: read your books in any voice — record a reference in the app, or import
a clip and let the app transcribe it. Install several TTS engines at once and switch
between them without restarting. Dutch voices (Piper ronnie and pim), plus Kokoro,
Kitten and ZipVoice. Opus, WAV and single-file M4B output with chapter marks.

Rendering now waits for a charger by default and pauses if the battery drops below 20%.
Import is guarded against hostile or oversized files, and progress is weighted by text
length rather than chapter count.
