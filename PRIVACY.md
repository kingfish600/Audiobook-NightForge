# Privacy Policy — Audiobook NightForge

Audiobook NightForge is fully offline.

- **No data collection.** The app has no analytics, no telemetry, no ads, and no network permission for user data.
- **Network use:** the only outbound connections are the model downloads you explicitly trigger (voice model files from the sherpa-onnx GitHub releases). Everything else — importing books, rendering audio, playback, exports — happens entirely on your device.
- **Your files:** imported books, rendered chapters, and exports live in the app's private storage or locations you choose via the Android file picker. Deleting the app removes them.
- **Crash reports** are written locally to your device only (`last-crash.txt`) and are shared by you, manually, if and when you choose to.

Contact: open an issue at https://github.com/kingfish600/Audiobook-NightForge
